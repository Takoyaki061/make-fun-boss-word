Entry for the [One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Meetingholic

The boss who calls a meeting about the meeting, then schedules a follow-up to discuss what the meeting decided. Every worker in every industry has sat in that meeting — which is why the word lands on its own, with no caption needed.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Stdlib only, deterministic.
